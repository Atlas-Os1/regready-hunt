# HuntClub Stewardship

Independent project. Club dues, scheduling, stand safety, workdays, harvest history, volunteer hours, and landowner renewal reports.

## Buyer
Private hunting clubs leasing property.

## MVP
Roster, dues status, hunt calendar, stand inspection, workday log, harvest log, landowner report.

## Monetization
$29/month small club or $490/year larger club.

## Gate before build
Sell a manual landowner renewal report to three clubs. Productize repeated fields only after payment.
