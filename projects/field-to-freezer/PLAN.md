# Field-to-Freezer

Independent project. Post-harvest checklist, official reporting links, CWD and transport guidance, processor discovery, booking requests, and freezer records.

## Buyer
Start with hunters and partner processors.

## MVP
One state, one species, verified checklist, processor directory, appointment request, receipt storage.

## Monetization
$5.99 season pass, processor SaaS, qualified processor referrals.

## Safety boundary
Official agency sources are authoritative. AI may explain sourced text but may not generate unsupported legal or food-safety instructions.
