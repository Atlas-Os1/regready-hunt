# Outfitter FieldOps

Independent project. Mobile trip-readiness and field operations for small guides and outfitters.

## Buyer
Solo outfitters and teams with 5 or fewer guides.

## MVP
Client intake, license/tag uploads, packing list, guide assignment, trip brief, offline notes, reminders, harvest/compliance records.

## Monetization
$59/month solo, $99/month team, payment fee later.

## Gate before build
Two paid preseason setup pilots and one live season workflow.
