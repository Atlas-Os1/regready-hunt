## Source-pack release rule

No Oklahoma rule is production-valid until the pack records:

- exact source URL;
- retrieval timestamp;
- effective season/year;
- species and unit scope;
- cited source section/page;
- reviewer;
- review status;
- stale/expiry check.

The current Worker intentionally reports `demo-data` and exposes the source URLs through `/api/rules/status`.
